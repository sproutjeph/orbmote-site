# Orbmote Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 14 November 2026

Orbmote ("we", "us") is a universal remote app for Android. This policy explains what
the app does with information about you and your devices. Orbmote is provided by
Mbah Jephthah Chisom, Nigeria ("the controller"). Contact:
**jephthah@deeepsolve.com**.

## The short version

- No account, no sign-in, no ads, no tracking, no analytics or advertising SDKs.
- Your rooms, TVs and settings stay on your phone.
- The only things that leave your phone for us or our service providers are your **purchase
  history** and a **random anonymous ID** used to unlock the app, handled by RevenueCat with
  Google Play.

## 1. What stays on your phone

To control your devices, Orbmote stores on your phone:

- the rooms you create and the devices in them: names you give them, brand and model, how each
  is reached (Wi-Fi protocol or infrared code set), the device's **IP address** on your network
  and its **MAC address** (used to wake a TV that is in standby);
- **pairing tokens** and client keys the TV gives Orbmote when you allow it, and the TV's
  **TLS certificate pin** (so the app can tell it is talking to the same TV next time);
- your settings (language, theme, haptics, which features you turned on).

This information is **never sent to us or to anyone else**. Orbmote talks to your TV, box or
air conditioner directly over your local network or by infrared. Pairing tokens, keys and
certificate pins are encrypted with a key kept in your phone's secure hardware and are never
included in backups; after moving to a new phone you pair each TV again.

**Android backup.** If backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may include the
rooms, device list and settings above in your phone's backup to **your own Google account**,
as for any app. We have no access to that backup. Pairing tokens, keys, certificate pins and
your purchase state are excluded.

## 2. Purchases: RevenueCat and Google Play

Orbmote is a paid app with a 3-day free trial on the yearly plan. Payments are made through
**Google Play**; we never see your card or payment details.

To know whether the app should be unlocked, Orbmote uses **RevenueCat** (RevenueCat, Inc.), a
service provider that processes data on our behalf:

- **Anonymous app user ID.** On first launch the RevenueCat SDK creates a random ID
  (`$RCAnonymousID:…`) on your phone. It is not linked to your name, email, Google account or
  advertising ID.
- **Purchase history.** Google Play's purchase records for Orbmote (which plan, the trial,
  renewals, cancellations, refunds, prices and currency, the store's order ID) are shared with
  RevenueCat so the app knows what you are entitled to.

We have configured RevenueCat **not** to collect device identifiers or advertising IDs, not to
set any custom attributes, and with its diagnostics turned off. RevenueCat's own policy:
https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy. Google's: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

**Why (legal basis):** to provide the app you bought (performance of a contract) and to keep
financial records the law requires (legal obligation).

The app keeps the last answer from the store on your phone so it keeps working offline for a
while (up to 7 days for a subscription, 30 days for Lifetime).

## 3. Permissions and what they are used for

Orbmote asks for each permission only when you use the feature that needs it.

| Permission | Used for | Not used for |
|---|---|---|
| **Nearby devices / local network** (`ACCESS_LOCAL_NETWORK`, Android 17+) | Finding your TVs and boxes on your Wi-Fi and sending them commands, waking them, reading volume/mute | Anything outside your home network; the addresses and names it sees stay on the phone |
| **Phone (call state)** (`READ_PHONE_STATE`), Android 12+ | Only "Mute TV during calls", if you turn it on: knowing that a call is ringing or has ended | Phone numbers, contacts or call logs: the app never reads them |
| **Notifications** | The lock-screen remote, the sleep timer and the one trial reminder | Marketing; we send no promotional notifications |
| **Alarms & reminders** (exact alarms) | Turning the TV off on time with the sleep timer | |
| **Infrared** (no prompt) | Sending IR codes from phones with an IR blaster | |

## 4. No ads, no tracking

Orbmote contains no advertising, no ad SDKs, no attribution or analytics SDKs, and no crash
reporting SDK. We do not sell or share personal information for advertising, and we do not
track you across apps or websites.

If we ever add anonymous diagnostics (for example which infrared code set worked), we will
update this policy and the Play Store listing first, and Settings will have a switch to turn
them off.

## 5. Sharing

We share information only with the service providers above (RevenueCat; Google as the store),
when the law requires it, or as part of a sale or merger of the app (you would be told first).
We do not sell your personal information.

## 6. How long we keep it

RevenueCat keeps purchase records for as long as the app is offered, or until you ask us to
delete them, except where tax or accounting law requires us to keep them longer. Data on your
phone is deleted when you uninstall Orbmote or clear its storage.

## 7. Your rights and deleting your data

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the data we hold about you, or object to its use,
by emailing **jephthah@deeepsolve.com**. Because there is no account, include the **order ID** from
your Google Play receipt (Play Store → profile → Payments & subscriptions → Budget & history),
so we can find your records. We will delete the matching RevenueCat customer record within
30 days and confirm by email. Google keeps its own purchase records under its policy; deleting
our records does not cancel a subscription (cancel it in Google Play, see the Terms).

You may also complain to your local data protection authority (in Nigeria, the Nigeria Data
Protection Commission).

## 8. Children

Orbmote is made for adults. It is not directed to children under 13 (under 16 in the EEA and
the UK), and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

## 9. Security and international transfers

Data sent to RevenueCat and Google is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). RevenueCat may process data
in the United States; transfers rely on the safeguards in RevenueCat's data processing terms (such as the EU
Standard Contractual Clauses).

## 10. Changes

We will post changes on this page and update the date above. Material changes will also be
shown in the app.
